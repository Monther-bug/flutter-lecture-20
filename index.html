App icons

What is an app icon, and why does it matter?

The picture that opens your app: on the Android launcher (home screen and app list) and the iOS home screen.

Also inSettings, notifications, the recent-apps screen and the app stores
The first thing users see, before any page of your app.

A good icon

  • simple
  • one idea
  • readable when tiny
  • same colors as the app

Android

Android adaptive icons

Foregroundthe logo on transparent
Backgrounda color: our blue

different Android phones cut different shapes:

circle
rounded square
squircle
iOS: always this

Since Android 8, an icon is two layers; the phone cuts the shape (the mask).

Only the middle of the foreground is always visible: the safe zone, a circle about 60 % of the width (dashed).
Old phones use a normal square icon, so we make both.
A logo that touches the edges gets cut off. Keep it small and centered in the foreground picture.

Configure

Configure it in pubspec.yaml

pubspec.yamlat the end, not inside flutter:
flutter_launcher_icons:
  android: true
  ios: true
  image_path: "assets/icon/app_icon.png"
  remove_alpha_ios: true
  adaptive_icon_background: "#0D47A1"
  adaptive_icon_foreground: "assets/icon/app_icon_foreground.png"
Indentation matters in YAML: the block starts at the left edge, with two spaces before each key.
Optional: min_sdk_android (default 21, fine to leave out) and adaptive_icon_foreground_inset (default 16 %, extra space around the foreground).
1android / ios: truereplace the default icon
2image_paththe full picture
3adaptive_icon_…adaptive_icon_background + adaptive_icon_foreground: the two Android layers (both are needed)
4remove_alpha_ios: truemakes sure iOS gets no transparency

On the phone

See the new icon on the phone

Icons are copied to the phone only when the app is installed.

Hot reload / hot restart do not change the icon.
  1. Stop the app
  2. Uninstall it from the emulator or phone
  3. flutter run again
Still the old icon? Restart the emulator; on iOS also delete the app and run again.

On Android, long-press the app icon → App info → Uninstall, or drag it to "Uninstall". Phone launchers keep icons in a cache, which is why uninstalling helps. Compare two emulators with different icon shapes to show the adaptive icon at work.

Bonus

Bonus: change the app's name

android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
<application
    android:label="Tripoli Shop"
    android:name="${applicationName}"
    android:icon="@mipmap/ic_launcher">
ios/Runner/Info.plist
<key>CFBundleDisplayName</key>
<string>Tripoli Shop</string>

This is the name under the icon (default: the project name, like course_app).

Stop and run again (uninstall if it does not change).
Change only android:label and CFBundleDisplayName, not the package name or CFBundleName.
Arabic works too: android:label="متجر طرابلس"
